People who scan a code

If you scan a QR code of the service, we ask you for nothing and set no cookies. We record the date, the country (derived from the IP address by Cloudflare's network) and the device type (phone, tablet or computer). The IP address is never stored. This data cannot identify you.

Service e-mails (confirmation, security, billing) are not advertising and have no unsubscribe link.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and to withdraw consent. From the Account page you can, without writing to us:

  • download all your data (JSON) and your codes (CSV);
  • delete the account and all associated data;
  • turn the marketing consent on or off.

For any other request, write to {{.ContactEmail}}; we answer within one month at most. If you think we have not handled your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency.

Minors

The service is for businesses and professionals of legal age. It is not aimed at minors and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy substantially, we will tell you by e-mail before it applies. The current version is always on this page.
